Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

The short version

  • This policy is for people covered by US consumer health data laws, such as Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada SB 370.
  • We use health data only to provide the features you ask for. We never sell it and never use it for advertising.
  • You can access, delete or withdraw consent for your health data at any time.

This summary helps you find your way around. The full text below is what applies.

1. Scope

This policy applies to "consumer health data" as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws in other US states. It supplements our Privacy Policy, which covers all the personal data we process. Kaizado is provided by Sissala Jesse Juhani (Finland).

2. Health data we collect and why

CategoryPurpose
Body measurements: height, weight, target weight and body-weight historyPlan training loads, calculate nutrition targets, show your progress
Injuries, limitations and answers to the safety questions (only with your consent)Avoid exercises that may be unsuitable and make your training safer
Workouts, training effort and estimated muscle fatigue and recoveryPlan and adapt your workouts and progression
Food, calorie, nutrient and water logs, and weekly check-insTrack your nutrition and adjust your targets
Anything health-related you write to the AI coachAnswer your questions

We collect this data directly from you when you enter it in the app, and derive some of it (such as recovery estimates) from what you log.

3. Who we share it with

We share consumer health data only with the processors that help us provide Kaizado, under contracts that limit their use of it:

  • our hosting provider (servers in the EU), to store it;
  • OpenAI, when you use the AI coach or AI workout generation, to produce the answer or workout;
  • our backup storage provider, which receives only encrypted backups it can't read;
  • our analytics provider, only if you allow usage analytics in the app. It learns which features you use (for example that you finished a workout or logged a meal) but not your measurements, health answers, food names or messages.

We do not sell consumer health data, and we don't share it with third parties for their own purposes or with affiliates. We disclose it to authorities only when the law requires it.

4. Your rights

  • Confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and access it, including a list of the third parties we share it with.
  • Withdraw your consent to our collection and sharing of your consumer health data.
  • Have your consumer health data deleted.

You can delete most data directly in the app, withdraw health consent in your body profile, or delete your whole account. For anything else, email contact@kaizado.com. We respond within 45 days. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; we'll respond to an appeal within 45 days. If you're not satisfied with the outcome of your appeal, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General.
