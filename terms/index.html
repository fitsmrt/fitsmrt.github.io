3. Health and safety

Kaizado is not a medical service. Workouts, weights, recovery estimates, calorie and macro targets and coach answers are general fitness information. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and they don't replace a doctor, physiotherapist, dietitian or qualified trainer who can assess you in person.

  • Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise or nutrition programme, especially if you are pregnant, have a heart, blood pressure, joint or other medical condition, are recovering from an injury, take medication, or have had an eating disorder.
  • Stop exercising immediately and get medical help if you feel pain, chest pain or pressure, dizziness, faintness or shortness of breath. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
  • Use proper technique, warm up, train within your limits, use equipment safely and have a spotter for heavy lifts. You decide what you actually do and how much weight you lift.
  • Calorie and macro targets are estimates based on the information you give. They aren't suitable for medical diets, and you shouldn't use Kaizado to manage a medical condition or an eating disorder.

Exercise involves a risk of injury. By using Kaizado you accept that risk to the extent the law allows. This doesn't limit our liability where the law doesn't allow it; see Liability.

4. AI features

The AI coach is artificial intelligence: when you chat with the coach you are talking to an AI system, not a person, and its answers are generated by models from OpenAI. Workouts and training plans are generated automatically from your data by Kaizado's training engine (rules that run on your phone, not OpenAI). Both answers and workouts:

  • can be inaccurate, incomplete, out of date or unsuitable for you, even when they sound confident;
  • are not professional medical, nutritional, psychological or legal advice;
  • should be checked with your own judgement, and with a professional when your health is involved.

Don't use the coach for emergencies, and don't share other people's personal information or sensitive details that aren't needed for your training. Please report replies that seem wrong or harmful using the report option in the app. Daily message limits apply to keep the service fair and affordable; the current limits are shown in the app.
