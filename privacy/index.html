The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to coach you: your account, the training and body details you enter, your workouts, food logs and coach chats.
  • We don't sell your data or show ads, and we don't track you across other apps. Your data is stored on our own servers in the EU.
  • If you allow it, the app sends us usage statistics, crash reports and screen recordings (with all text and images hidden) so we can fix bugs and improve Kaizado. It's off until you say yes, and you can turn it off at any time in Preferences.
  • Workouts and training plans are built on your phone by Kaizado's own training engine; nothing is sent to an AI company for that. When you chat with the AI coach, the relevant parts of your profile and training history are sent to OpenAI to produce the answer. OpenAI doesn't use it to train its models.
  • Injuries and health screening answers are optional and used only with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • You can see, correct, export or delete your data. Deleting your account in the app removes it straight away.

This summary helps you find your way around. The full text below is what applies.

1. Who we are

Kaizado is an AI personal trainer app for Android and iOS, together with this website (kaizado.com). Kaizado is provided by Sissala Jesse Juhani, a sole trader registered in Tampere, Finland, business ID 3534911-4, VAT ID FI35349114 ("Kaizado", "we", "us"). We are the controller of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Contact us about anything in this policy at contact@kaizado.com. Kaizado is run by one independent developer, so we haven't appointed a data protection officer; privacy questions go straight to the person responsible for the app.

This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it and what your rights are. Our Terms of Service explain the rules for using Kaizado. If you live in a US state with a consumer health data law, also read our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

2. What we collect

Account

  • Your email address, username and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple: the email address and, where you allow it, the name those services share with us. We never see your Google or Apple password.

Training profile and body details

  • Gender, date of birth, height, weight, target weight and target date, units.
  • Your goal, experience level, equipment, preferred split, training days, session length, daily activity level and nutrition targets.

Health details (optional)

  • Injuries or limitations (for example knee or lower back) and your answers to the safety questions (for example heart conditions, chest pain, dizziness or pregnancy). We only store these if you give explicit consent; see Health data.

Training and nutrition

  • Workouts you create and complete: exercises, sets, reps, weights, reps in reserve, ratings, duration, total volume and estimated calories.
  • Training preferences, such as exercises you exclude or favour, and planned deload weeks.
  • Food and drink you log (food name, brand, amount, calories and nutrients), water intake, body weight entries and weekly check-ins.

AI coach

  • Your coach chats and the replies, chat titles, and the coach notes you choose to save.
  • If you report an AI reply, the reason you give and a copy of the reported text.

Community

  • Your public profile (username, profile picture and bio), posts, photos, comments and likes.
  • Reports you make about other people's content, and reports others make about yours.

Purchases

  • If you subscribe to Premium: which plan you have, its status and renewal dates, and the store's transaction identifiers. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store; we never receive your card details.

Messages to us

  • What you write when you email us, and your email address.

Technical data

  • Our servers log each request: IP address, time, the part of the service called, the result and how long it took. We use these logs to keep the service running and secure, and to enforce fair-use limits.
  • The app does not contain advertising or attribution SDKs, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Usage analytics and crash reports (only if you allow it)

  • When you first use the app we ask whether you want to share usage data. If you say yes, the app sends PostHog (see Who we share data with):
    • which screens you open and which features you use, for example starting or finishing a workout, logging food, sending a coach message or opening the Premium page, with simple details such as the number of sets or the meal type;
    • crash and error reports, including where in the app they happened;
    • screen recordings of how you move through the app. All text and images are hidden in the recordings, so they don't show what you type, your photos or your data;
    • your device model, operating system, app version, language and time zone, and your approximate country, which is looked up from your IP address. The IP address itself isn't stored;
    • a random identifier and, once you sign in, your Kaizado user ID, plus a few training settings (your goal, experience level, equipment, training days, units and whether you have Premium).
  • It does not receive your name, email address, body measurements, injury or health answers, food names, chat messages, posts or photos.
  • You can change your answer at any time in Profile → Preferences → Share usage data. When you turn it off, the app stops sending immediately.

Stored only on your device

  • Settings (theme, language, units, reminders), a workout that's in progress, workouts waiting to sync, recently used foods, cached images and food lookups, and whether you've accepted the AI and community notices. Training reminders are scheduled on your device; we don't use push notification services.
  • When you sign out, the app removes the data about your account from the device. Only workouts that haven't synced yet stay, and they upload the next time you sign in with the same account.

4. Health data

Details about your health are a special category of personal data under the GDPR. Some of what you enter in Kaizado, such as injuries, your answers to the safety questions and, depending on the context, your body weight, can count as health data.

  • We only use health data with your explicit consent, which you give in the app before entering injuries or answering the safety questions. It is used only to make your training safer and better suited to you.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time in your body profile. When you do, we delete your injury and safety answers. Withdrawing doesn't affect what we did before, and you can keep using Kaizado without them.
  • We never use health data for advertising and never sell or share it for anyone else's marketing.

5. Workout engine, AI coach and OpenAI

Workouts and multi-week training plans are built by Kaizado's training engine, a set of evidence-based rules that runs on your phone and uses your profile, plan, workout history and feedback. It doesn't use OpenAI or any other AI company. Your plan and the workouts it creates are stored in your account like the rest of your training data.

The AI coach chat is powered by large language models from OpenAI. When you send the coach a message, our server (never the app itself) sends OpenAI what the model needs to give you a useful answer. If you ask the coach for a workout, the coach asks the training engine to build it; the workout itself isn't written by OpenAI. What is sent:

  • Your first name or username, gender, age, height, weight and BMI, goal, targets, experience, equipment and schedule.
  • Injuries and safety answers, but only if you've given health consent.
  • Your saved coach notes, nutrition targets, today's food and water totals and your latest body weight.
  • Your recent workouts (up to the last 10, with sets, reps and weights) and estimated muscle fatigue.
  • Your message and up to the 20 previous messages in the same chat.

We don't send your email address. To help OpenAI detect abuse we send a one-way hashed identifier instead of your user ID. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API isn't used to train its models and is kept by OpenAI for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, after which it is deleted.

We store your chats so you can come back to them. You can delete a chat at any time, which deletes its messages. AI output is automatically generated and can be wrong; see our Terms of Service.

6. Community

Anything you post in the community, along with your username, profile picture and bio, can be seen by other Kaizado users. Photos you post are stored at web addresses that anyone who has the link can open. Only post what you're comfortable sharing.

If someone reports content, we review it together with the report and may remove it or restrict an account, as described in our Terms of Service. We tell you why if we act on your content.

10. How we protect your data

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). The app refuses unencrypted connections.
  • Database access rules ensure each account can only read and change its own private data.
  • Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. Backups are encrypted.
  • Access to production systems is limited to the people who run Kaizado and protected with strong authentication.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we'll tell you and the supervisory authority as the law requires.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR (and similar laws elsewhere) you have the right to:

  • Access your data and get a copy of it.
  • Correct data that is wrong or incomplete. You can edit most of it yourself in the app.
  • Delete your data. You can delete chats, posts and your whole account in the app.
  • Port your data: receive it in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON) or have it sent to another service.
  • Restrict or object to processing that relies on our legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example for health data in your body profile or for usage analytics in Preferences.

To use a right we can't handle in the app, email contact@kaizado.com from the address linked to your account. We reply within one month and may ask you to confirm your identity. Requests are free unless they are clearly unfounded or excessive.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Finland that is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman; you can also contact the authority where you live or work. We'd appreciate the chance to sort things out with you first.

12. Additional information for US residents

Several US states (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and Washington) give residents rights over their personal data. The categories of data we collect, the sources, the purposes and the recipients are described above.

  • We do not sell personal data, "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising, or use it for targeted advertising or profiling with significant effects.
  • We only use sensitive personal data (such as health information) to provide the features you ask for.
  • You can ask to know, access, correct or delete your data, and you won't be treated differently for using these rights. Authorised agents can submit requests with your signed permission.
  • If we turn down your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and we'll respond within 45 days.

For consumer health data under Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws, see our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

13. Children

Kaizado isn't meant for children. You must be at least 13 to use the app, and the app doesn't accept a younger date of birth. In some countries you need to be older to consent to data processing yourself (for example 16), and below that age you need a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.
