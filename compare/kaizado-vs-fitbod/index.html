Kaizado vs Fitbod

Facts checked on November 11, 2026

Fitbod and Kaizado both generate your workouts and progress the weights automatically. Fitbod has a larger exercise video library and a longer track record; Kaizado adds a free version, an AI coach chat, nutrition tracking and multi-week plans to a goal date.

Choose Kaizado if…

  • You want to start for free, without a trial deadline.
  • You want an AI coach to talk to and nutrition tracking in the same app.
  • You want a week-by-week plan to a goal date, not only the next workout.
  • You want the app in one of 12 languages.

Choose Fitbod if…

  • You want the biggest library of exercise videos filmed from several angles.
  • You need an iPhone app today.
  • You prefer an established app with hundreds of thousands of ratings.

What Kaizado does differently

  • A free version. Log workouts, build your own and follow the first week of a plan without paying. Fitbod has a 7-day trial and then needs a subscription.
  • An AI coach. Ask questions about your training, recovery or food and get answers based on your own history.
  • Nutrition in the same app. Barcode and AI photo food logging, with calorie targets that adjust from your weigh-ins. Try the free calorie calculator.
  • Plans to a goal date. Kaizado builds a week-by-week plan with planned deload weeks, not only the next workout.
  • Lower price. Kaizado Premium costs less per month and per year than Fitbod in the US.

Where Fitbod is stronger

  • Exercise videos. Fitbod has more than 1,000 exercises with multi-angle videos. Kaizado has 1,300+ exercises with instructions and animations, but not filmed videos for every one.
  • Track record. Fitbod has hundreds of thousands of App Store ratings and years of refinement behind its workout engine.
  • Platforms. Fitbod is on iPhone and Apple Watch today; Kaizado’s iPhone app is coming soon.

Switching from Fitbod

  1. Kaizado asks about your goal, experience, schedule and equipment when you start, then builds your first plan.
  2. If you have workout history in another app that exports Strong or Hevy CSV files, import it under Profile → Import workouts so your starting weights match your strength.

Questions

Is Kaizado better than Fitbod?

Both generate workouts and adjust your weights. Fitbod has more exercise videos and a longer history; Kaizado adds a free version, an AI coach, nutrition tracking and plans to a goal date at a lower price.

Is there a free Fitbod alternative?

Kaizado has a free version with workout logging, your own workouts, the first week of a plan and a few AI coach messages a day. Premium adds generated workouts for every session.

Does Fitbod have an AI coach?

Fitbod generates workouts with its own algorithm. As of November 2026 we found no AI chat coach in the app.

Which is cheaper, Fitbod or Kaizado?

In the US, Kaizado Premium is $59.99 a year and Fitbod is $95.99 a year (App Store and Google Play prices checked November 2026).
