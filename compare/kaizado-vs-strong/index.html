Kaizado vs Strong

Facts checked on November 11, 2026

Strong is a simple, fast workout logger loved for staying out of your way; Kaizado is an AI personal trainer that plans your workouts, adjusts your weights and tracks nutrition. Choose Strong for pure logging, Kaizado if you want the planning done for you.

Choose Kaizado if…

  • You want workouts planned for your goal, time, equipment and recovery.
  • You want weights that adjust automatically after every set.
  • You want an AI coach, nutrition tracking and a community in the same app.
  • You train on Android, with or without a Wear OS watch.

Choose Strong if…

  • You want the simplest possible logger and plan your own training.
  • You want an Apple Watch app that works without your phone, today.
  • You like a one-time lifetime purchase.

What Kaizado does differently

  • It plans your training. Kaizado builds each workout and a week-by-week plan for your goal; Strong expects you to bring your own program, like a push pull legs or upper lower split.
  • It adjusts the weights. After each set Kaizado sets the next load from your reps and effort, and plans deloads.
  • Coach and nutrition built in. An AI coach that knows your history, plus food logging and adaptive calorie targets.
  • Unlimited own workouts for free. Kaizado’s free version has no limit on the workouts you build yourself.

Where Strong is stronger

  • Simplicity. Strong does one thing, logging, and does it with very little friction. Kaizado has more features, which means more to learn.
  • Apple Watch. Strong’s Apple Watch app works without the phone today. Kaizado’s Apple Watch app arrives with the iPhone app.
  • Price and lifetime option. Strong PRO is cheaper and can be bought once for life.

Switching from Strong

  1. In Strong, export your data as a CSV file from the app’s settings.
  2. In Kaizado, open Profile → Import workouts and choose the Strong file. Your history, records and estimated maxes come across.
  3. Kaizado then suggests starting weights from your Strong history.

Questions

Is Kaizado better than Strong?

Strong is better if you want a minimal logger and plan your own training. Kaizado is better if you want the app to plan your workouts, adjust your weights and track nutrition.

Can I import my Strong workouts into Kaizado?

Yes. Export a CSV from Strong and import it in Kaizado under Profile → Import workouts.

Is Strong free?

Strong has a free version with unlimited workouts and up to 3 custom routines. Strong PRO removes the limit.

Does Strong have AI workouts?

No. Strong is a logger: you build your own routines. Kaizado generates workouts and plans with AI.
